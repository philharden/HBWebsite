Solution Architecture

Definition of system structure, technology, and security strategy.

Overview

The Solution Architecture phase defines the structural and technical strategy of the system. Hardened Builds LLC designs architectures that are secure by default, maintainable over their operational life, and portable across hosting environments. Where artificial intelligence components are present, the architecture is designed to minimize model token consumption while preserving durable and consistent results.

Activities and Deliverables

  • Architecture, component, and data-flow diagrams.
  • Technology selection with documented justification and trade-off analysis.
  • Security architecture incorporating default-deny authorization and defense in depth.
  • Integration strategy for internal systems and third-party services.
  • Token-minimization strategy for artificial intelligence components, favoring retrieval-augmented generation with vector search, response caching, deterministic short-circuits, schema-constrained model calls, and routing to smaller models in preference to transmitting large context.

Phase Specifics

  • Architecture and component diagrams
  • Technology selection and integration strategy
  • Security and compliance design
  • Token-efficient artificial intelligence design

Engagement

This phase is available as part of a complete lifecycle engagement or as a discrete service.
