Post-deployment HyperCare

Heightened support during the stabilization period.

Overview

The HyperCare phase provides heightened, time-bounded support during the critical period immediately following deployment. Hardened Builds LLC assigns a dedicated response team to ensure rapid identification and resolution of issues while the solution stabilizes in production.

Activities and Deliverables

  • A dedicated response team for the defined stabilization period.
  • Priority incident management with expedited response commitments.
  • Expedited resolution of defects affecting production operation.
  • Regular reporting on system stability and outstanding items.
  • A documented transition from HyperCare to ongoing support.

Phase Specifics

  • Dedicated response team
  • Priority incident management
  • Expedited defect resolution
  • Regular stabilization reporting

Engagement

This phase is available as part of a complete lifecycle engagement or as a discrete service.
